# Research Notes

**Indian Digital Scam Intelligence & Prevention System (ScamShield)**

Use this folder for:

- **Related-work notes** and the research gap statement (`gap.md`)
- **Literature review** - papers, tools, and existing solutions to compare against
- **Evaluation tables** - baseline model results, comparative metrics
- **Unseen demo examples** - real-world test cases for the prototype
- **Limitations and future scope** - honest assessment of what the system does not do

## Research principles

The priority is research evidence, not a flashy frontend:

1. Dataset quality
2. Correct labels
3. Reproducible preprocessing
4. Baseline ML
5. Proper evaluation
6. Explainability
7. Risk scoring
8. URL analysis
9. QR analysis
10. User interface
11. Advanced scam-chain detection

The project should use academically cautious language: *research gap*, *proposed investigation*, *comparative evaluation*, *baseline*, *experimental evaluation*, *limitations*.

Do not make unsupported claims. Do not claim the system is complete or production-ready until the evidence supports it.

## Privacy

Do not put private messages or real victim data here. Only sanitised or synthetic examples.
